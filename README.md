# Olist E-Commerce Business Analytics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the Brazilian Olist E-Commerce dataset to understand business performance, customer behavior, product performance, payment patterns, seller performance, and delivery operations.

The objective is to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights, identify potential problems, investigate their root causes, and support data-driven decision-making.

This is an end-to-end Business Analytics project using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall e-commerce sales and business performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior and repeat customers.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming product categories.
- Analyze seller performance and revenue contribution.
- Investigate payment methods and payment patterns.
- Evaluate delivery performance and its relationship with customer reviews.
- Identify business problems and provide actionable recommendations.

## Business Questions

1. How are orders and revenue trending over time?
2. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
3. Which product categories receive the highest number of orders?
4. What is the customer repeat purchase rate?
5. Which sellers contribute the most to marketplace revenue?
6. Which payment methods are most commonly used?
7. How frequently are orders delivered late?
8. How does delivery performance affect customer review scores?
9. What business areas require improvement?

- ## Business Insights & Recommendations

### Key Business Insights

**1. Low Customer Retention**
- The repeat customer rate was approximately **3.0%**.
- Most customers placed only one order, indicating an opportunity to improve customer retention and encourage repeat purchases.

**Business Recommendation:** Introduce loyalty programs, personalized offers, targeted email campaigns, and incentives for repeat purchases.

---

**2. Delivery Performance Impacts Customer Satisfaction**
- Orders delivered late received an average review score of approximately **2.27**.
- Orders delivered on time or early received an average review score of approximately **4.29**.
- This indicates a strong relationship between delivery performance and customer satisfaction.

**Business Recommendation:** Improve delivery monitoring, identify sellers and regions with frequent delays, and take corrective action to improve fulfillment performance.

---

**3. Revenue Concentration Among Top Sellers**
- The top 10 sellers generated approximately **13.15% of total marketplace revenue**.
- Seller performance varies, making seller-level monitoring important for understanding revenue contribution.

**Business Recommendation:** Identify high-performing sellers, understand the factors driving their success, and support underperforming sellers through operational improvements.

---

**4. Average Order Value vs. Median Order Value**
- Average Order Value (AOV) was approximately **R$137.75**.
- Median Order Value was approximately **R$86.90**.
- The difference suggests that higher-value orders influence the average order value.

**Business Recommendation:** Analyze high-value purchases, product combinations, and customer purchasing patterns to identify opportunities for cross-selling and upselling.

---

**5. Payment Method Preferences**
- Credit cards represented the most frequently used payment method.
- Boleto was another significant payment method, while voucher and debit card usage were comparatively lower.

**Business Recommendation:** Optimize the checkout experience around popular payment methods while evaluating opportunities to improve adoption of alternative payment options.

---

### Business Recommendations Summary

| Business Area | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Customer Retention | Loyalty programs and targeted campaigns to encourage repeat purchases |
| Delivery Operations | Monitor late deliveries and investigate problematic sellers and regions |
| Seller Performance | Benchmark sellers and support underperforming sellers |
| Revenue Growth | Explore cross-selling, upselling, and high-value customer segments |
